## GPU proof: correctness

Prompt "The capital of France is", tokenizer ids printed by the
runner before any weight work:

```text
prompt_ids=[200000, 954, 7963, 323, 11698, 373]
coverage: expected=731 found=731 missing=0
generated_ids=[13796, 26, 1573, 373, 290, 2167, 26899, 1212]
generated_text=" Paris. It is the most populous"
```

All 8 generated ids match the verified reference
[13796, 26, 1573, 373, 290, 2167, 26899, 1212] exactly. First-token
probability on the GPU: 0.403462 (CPU-verified stack: 0.4035;
llama.cpp reference: 0.3962). The 64-token continuation on the same
prompt is coherent text (" Paris. It is the most populous city in
France and the most populous city proper in the European Union. The
Paris Region had a population of 12,405,426 in January 2023, ..."),
so the GPU forward is not merely argmax-lucky on one prompt shape.

## GPU proof: performance (first composed forward, honestly read)

| Phase | Measured |
| --- | --- |
| File open + parse | 4.9 s |
| Host weight load (itak-loader) | 4.6 s |
| Backend open (device + pipelines) | 0.02 s |
| First forward = prefill of 6 tokens, incl. one-time upload of the full 15.6 GiB weight set to the card | 3.15 s (8-token run), 3.27 s (64-token run) |
| Wall clock, process start to first token | ~12.7 s |
| Decode, 64-token run, steps 3..64 (n=62) | mean 505.8 ms/token, median 495 ms, min 475, max 621 → **1.98 tok/s** |
| Peak VRAM during the 64-token run | 17,332,375,552 bytes (16.1 GiB) over the 0.11 GB idle baseline: the full weight set resident plus KV and scratch |
| Peak host RSS | 33,093,324 KB (~31.6 GiB); the host keeps its own copy of the weights |

Against the llama.cpp Vulkan baselines on this card (998.43 tok/s
prefill, 44.31 tok/s decode solo), the composed GPU forward is at
1.98 tok/s decode, roughly 22x slower, and its 6-token prefill is
dominated by the one-time residency upload, so no prefill rate is
quoted against the 998 anchor.

The gap is composition, not kernels, and the mechanism is visible in
the crate contracts. The K1/K2/K3 GEMV kernels bench at 816.5 /
831.9 / 813.2 GB/s in isolation (BACKEND.md), at or above llama.cpp's
708 GB/s effective stream. But the `Compute` trait as wired returns
host `Vec<f32>` values: every projection's output round-trips to the
host, and the attention step reads the rotated K back once per layer
per token so the host mirror holds what the GPU computed. A decode
token is ~700 blocking submissions and readbacks; the measured
475-621 ms band is that latency, flat with position, which is why the
rate does not degrade as the context grows. The fix direction is
already named in the tree: device-resident activations end to end
(the K4 crate carries a fused `norm_residual` entry point kept "for
the future device-resident forward"), with host readback only for the
final logits. Until that lands, the GPU backend is a correctness
oracle at serving-incompatible speed, exactly what this proof was
scoped to establish.

## DFlash depth sweep (llama.cpp, reference stack)

Protocol per SPEC-itak-spec.md Section 9, temperature-0 arm: a
temporary llama-server (build-vulkan2 binaries, commit a7af149) on
port 18923 (18099 turned out to be occupied by an unrelated service;
its /health answered 200 and its /completion 404'd, which killed the
first sweep attempt before any measurement), solo `-np 1`, `-ngl
999`, raw prompts (no chat template), `n_predict` 256 with
`ignore_eos`, 3 fixed prompts from bench/accuracy-prompts.md (MATH,
GO_CODE, FACTUAL), 1 warmup + 3 measured reps per prompt, server
restarted per depth so the per-request draft counters start clean.
Rates are the server timings' eval tok/s; acceptance is the timings'
per-request `draft_n_accepted / draft_n`, aggregated over the 9
measured requests per cell (2,304 emitted tokens per cell).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| plain | 41.64 | 1.00x | n/a | n/a |
| 1 | 61.36 | 1.47x | 1057/1232 = 0.858 | 0.85 |

Per-prompt medians worth noting: MATH peaks at depth 6 (107.5) a
hair over depth 4 (106.8); FACTUAL peaks at depth 4 (96.1) a hair
over depth 6 (95.2); GO_CODE is the noisy cell (depth 6 runs spread
104-143, median 138.3). Run spreads elsewhere were tight (most cells
within ~2 tok/s across reps).

**Operating point: depth 6.** SPEC 9.4's rule (smallest depth within
2% of the best median): the best median is depth 6 at 107.39, the 2%
band floor is 105.24, and depth 4 at 103.01 sits 4.1% under the best,
outside the band, so 6 is the pick on this harness. It is a soft win:
depth 4 is within noise of 6 on two of three prompts and remains the
recorded production profile; the honest statement is that the curve
is flat between 4 and 6 and falls off a cliff at 8.

**Depths 8 and up are dead weight on this stack.** Accepted tokens
per request saturate from depth 6 onward (1,749 → 1,755 → 1,781 →
1,866 across depths 6/8/12/16) while proposed tokens grow linearly,
so each round verifies 8-16 positions to bank ~3.2-4.3 tokens, and
wall time at depth 8 drops below the plain non-speculative rate.
The draft's useful horizon on these prompts is about 3 accepted
tokens per round; the pasted article's depth-16 claim (75 to 110+
tok/s, REFERENCES.md) is not reproduced: depth 16 measured 49.97
median, and even the best depth (6) only just reaches the article's
band at 107.39.

**Anchor finding (a finding, not a rounding error, per RUNBOOK).**
The harness did not reproduce the recorded anchors within a few
percent: plain measured 41.64 against the recorded 44.31 (-6.0%),
and depth 4 measured 103.01 against the recorded 88.6 (+16.3%), so
the recorded 2.00x ratio becomes 2.47x here. The recorded numbers
came from different harness shapes (llama-bench tg128 after a
512-token prefill for the 44.31; the 2026-10-28 model-suite run for
the 88.6), while this sweep reads llama-server /completion eval
rates on short raw prompts with fixed 256-token generations. The
sweep is internally consistent (one binary, one prompt set, one
discipline for every cell), so the operating point stands; but the
recorded anchors are harness-relative, and any future comparison of
our stack against "44.31 / 88.6" must either re-run this harness or
re-baseline. A DECISIONS.md entry records this in the same pass, per
SPEC 9.4. Acceptance is reported as cell totals because llama-server
timings expose no per-position breakdown; per-position acceptance
(SPEC 9.3) remains measurable only on our own stack's diag events.
